Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 19, 2026

Your privacy is paramount at Formulaw. This policy explains how we collect, protect, and use your personal information when you use our legal consultation services.

1. Information we collect

To provide high-quality legal matching, we collect specific data points including:

  • Identity Data: Full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Case Data: Information you share regarding your legal query to help us match you with the right expert.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, and platform usage metrics.

2. How we use your data

We process your information to fulfill our contract with you, specifically to:

  • Create and manage your Formulaw account.
  • Facilitate direct communication with your assigned legal expert.
  • Process secure payments and prevent platform fraud.
  • Send essential updates about your consultation status.

3. Data Sharing

Formulaw does not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share information with your assigned legal expert and our secure payment processors. In exceptional cases, we may disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your information. While no system is 100% secure, we continuously update our practices to mitigate risks and protect your sensitive documentation.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. You may also opt-out of non-essential communications at any time through your account settings.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, data requests, or concerns, please reach out to our Data Protection Officer at [email protected].
